Nenterprises Group Structure

A parent institution with constitutional divisions and commercial subsidiaries.

Nenterprises (Pty) Ltd serves as the ownership vehicle, governing layer, and parent institution for the broader ecosystem.

Version

1.0 | June 2026

Divisions are internal infrastructure. Subsidiaries are marketplace-facing companies. Capital, doctrine, and strategic attention remain under the parent institution.

Parent Entity

Nenterprises (Pty) Ltd

Nenterprises owns, develops, governs, and supports ecosystem divisions, intellectual property, and subsidiary companies.

Divisions

Institutional infrastructure inside the parent organization.

These entities operate as divisions of Nenterprises and are not separate legal entities unless formally established otherwise.

First Conviction

Formation and self-stewardship infrastructure

Develops individuals from unexamined living toward intentional living and self-stewardship.

Accord Signal

Capability development infrastructure

Builds readiness through conditioning, certification, role architecture, and attention systems.

Steward State

Institutional stewardship infrastructure

Develops people capable of carrying responsibilities, institutions, missions, and authority beyond themselves.

City of Problem Solving

Problem selection and capital allocation infrastructure

Identifies meaningful problems, evaluates opportunities, and determines where attention and capital should be allocated.

Idea Gravity

Meaning and narrative infrastructure

Articulates, strengthens, and communicates ideas capable of attracting people, communities, capital, and institutions.

Subsidiaries

Commercial execution carried into the marketplace.

Commercial subsidiaries generate revenue through marketplace activity while Nenterprises allocates budgets, resources, personnel, and strategic attention.

Response Integrity (Pty) Ltd

Education and performance conditioning

Helps students develop stable academic performance under pressure through mathematics mentorship and response conditioning systems.

Relief Works Technologies (Pty) Ltd

Execution infrastructure

Builds systems, software, automation, documentation, and operational infrastructure that reduce fragility and improve execution.

Tsa Kasi Logistics (Pty) Ltd

Commerce movement infrastructure

Builds regional logistics infrastructure that enables reliable, affordable, and scalable movement of goods, services, and commerce.

Capital Structure Principle

Divisions do not hold capital independently.

Nenterprises allocates budgets, resources, personnel, and attention to divisions according to strategic priorities and ecosystem needs.

Ownership Principle

Institutional assets remain under the parent entity.

Ecosystem constitutions, methodologies, brands, doctrines, and institutional systems are owned by Nenterprises unless formally assigned by written resolution.
